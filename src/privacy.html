Privacy Policy

What leaves your machine, and what stays in your repository.

This policy describes the arnx CLI, arnx cloud, and this website, reviewed on October 22, 2026. {{CLI_DISTRIBUTION_STATUS}} ARNX is the operator of arnx.dev; contact info@arnx.dev about privacy or data requests.

Your own keys and arnx Cloud

With your own API keys, inference requests go from the CLI to the providers configured in .arnx/config.toml. Those providers receive the request content under your account and their API terms. BYOM does not mean all processing stays on your machine.

{{CLOUD_DESCRIPTION}} {{CLOUD_DATA_FLOW}} Read the Cloud service terms before sharing code. Contact info@arnx.dev for provider, retention, or data-handling questions specific to your use.

arnx cloud accounts

Sign-in goes through GitHub. We receive your GitHub account ID, login, and email address if shared. Your credit balance and its ledger are kept per account.

Payments are processed by Stripe, which sends receipts and invoices by email. Card numbers go directly to Stripe; arnx never sees them. Top-ups open soon.

Review content passes through arnx cloud to its model providers. After a review, arnx cloud keeps account-linked review records, including status, timestamps, and billed usage by role, alongside the credit ledger. For questions about review-content retention, backups, or model providers’ handling of your data, contact info@arnx.dev.

What can be submitted to a model

  • The diff selected for review.
  • Source files the reviewer reads to understand the change.
  • Generated tests and their execution output.
  • Answers you supply about intended behavior, when used to generate tests.

.arnxignore and secret-file/content filters exclude recognized sensitive material. Filtering is not exhaustive. arnx allow-secret can allow particular key-shaped content; do not use it to expose real secrets.

Setup and review normally omit untracked files in repositories with commits. --untracked or [review] untracked = true includes them, and --no-untracked overrides that setting. With no commits yet, non-ignored files form the initial change. Inspect the scope before proceeding.

What remains local

Tests execute in Docker against a repository copy. Image builds can use the network; test runs have no external network, or an internal network for configured services. The CLI stores configuration, reviews, generated tests, acceptance records, and usage logs under .arnx/. Accepted tests are copied into project test directories. You control their retention and whether to commit them.

These reports can contain code and provider responses. Sanitize them before sharing. Read the execution boundaries.

This website

  • Light mode is the default. If you switch themes, this website saves your choice in your browser’s local storage so it persists between visits.
  • arnx.dev is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare, which process connection information to deliver pages.
  • The site uses Google Analytics 4 for page views and curated clicks on GitHub, docs, guided-pilot links, and successful install-command copies when installation is available. Event fields describe UI locations and actions; they do not include form content, repository source, private repository identifiers, or email-link contents. Advertising features are disabled.
  • For visitors in the EU, EEA, UK, or an unknown location, the analytics script loads only after Accept. A Cloudflare trace request determines the country; the site does not store the country. Your cookie choice is kept in this browser.
  • Outside those regions, analytics loads after the page. A browser content blocker can prevent it. Where the consent controls are shown, use Cookies in the footer to change your choice; Reject also removes analytics cookies.
  • If you email us, we receive your address and message and use them to respond. Do not include private code or credentials.

Privacy requests

Contact info@arnx.dev to request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal data held by ARNX. For data held under your own model-provider account, contact that provider. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Processing by hosting, analytics, and model providers can occur outside your country.
