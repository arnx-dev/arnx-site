Privacy Policy

What arnx sends, what we keep, and for how long.

This policy is from ARNX (“arnx”, “we”, “us”). It covers the arnx command-line tool, arnx cloud and this website. Our Terms of Service cover the rest.

Two ways to use arnx

  • With your own keys. The open-source arnx tool runs on your machine. Your code goes from your machine straight to the model providers named in .arnx/config.toml (Anthropic, OpenAI, River or Z.ai), under your key and that provider’s terms. We receive none of it.
  • With arnx cloud (“arnx models”). Your code goes to our server at https://api.arnx.dev. Our server calls the model providers with our keys. arnx cloud is in an early pilot.

What arnx sends to a model

Either way, a review sends:

  • the change you are reviewing (its diff);
  • the files the reviewer reads to understand it;
  • the tests arnx writes, and their results.

arnx does not send:

  • files that usually hold secrets, such as .env files, key files and credential files;
  • text that looks like a key, unless you allow the file that holds it with arnx allow-secret (for example, test fixtures);
  • paths you list in .arnxignore;
  • untracked files, unless you opt in (--untracked) or the repository has no commits yet.

Tests run in Docker on your own machine, on a copy of your code. They get none of your environment variables (only ones you declare for the tests), and no network access beyond the services you set up for them. With arnx cloud, your model keys are not needed and never sent.

What arnx cloud keeps

  • Your account. From GitHub sign-in: your GitHub user id, your username, and your primary verified email (if GitHub shares one). GitHub’s access token is used once and never stored. At sign-in the arnx tool also sends your computer’s name and the arnx version; we keep them with your sign-in.
  • Secrets as hashes only. Sign-in tokens and device codes are stored only as hashes, never in readable form.
  • Credits and usage. A ledger of credits, charges and refunds. For each review: its effort level, status, times, and cost per role (for example, the reviewer or the second opinion).
  • Review content, only while a review runs. So a server restart does not lose your review (or a setup run), we keep its state encrypted in our database. It is deleted when the review finishes, and in any case after one hour without activity.
  • Daily totals of model calls, tokens and cost per provider and model, with no account, code or prompt in them. We use them to check our bills.
  • No code in logs. Our server logs record ids, counts, sizes and amounts. They never contain your prompts or code.
  • Backups. The database is backed up nightly. Backups are kept for 14 days, then deleted.

Account and ledger data are kept while your account is open, and then for 180 days, unless the law requires us to keep payment records longer. The ledger is a financial record, so its entries are never edited.

Payments

When you buy credits, Stripe handles the payment. We send Stripe your account id and email. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. Paid credits are not switched on yet.

Telemetry (optional)

Telemetry is off unless you say yes. The current arnx release does not collect it yet: it does not ask and sends nothing. Once it is switched on, it works like this.

  • arnx asks once per machine. Your answer is stored in ~/.config/arnx/telemetry.json (or under $XDG_CONFIG_HOME), never in your repository. A teammate who clones the repository sends nothing because of your answer.
  • If you say yes, arnx makes a random install id. If you say no, or turn it off later, the id is deleted.
  • arnx telemetry shows what would be sent. arnx telemetry off stops it. Setting ARNX_TELEMETRY=0 or DO_NOT_TRACK=1 turns it off whatever you chose.
  • Sending never slows or breaks a command: one short try, and errors are ignored.

After setup, review, accept and check, arnx sends one line to https://api.arnx.dev/v1/telemetry with:

  • the random install id, the arnx version, your OS and CPU type, the command, and the time;
  • how many projects, and their languages;
  • how many files changed, and the size of the diff;
  • the effort level;
  • the model providers used (not the models);
  • how many findings and suspects, the second opinion’s verdicts, and the before/after results;
  • how many tests were written, passed, flaky or erroring, and how many you kept;
  • your marks as counts (real, intended, noise, unmarked; chosen or left as they came);
  • how many questions you answered;
  • cost, duration and the exit code.

Never sent:

  • code or diffs;
  • file names or paths;
  • test names or test content;
  • messages;
  • the questions, or your answers;
  • repository names or remotes;
  • user names;
  • environment variables;
  • keys.

Our server refuses any field not on that list. It does not keep your IP address or request headers, and does not link telemetry to an arnx cloud account. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. Before that, each week is rolled into totals with no install id, and those totals are kept.

Saying yes or no changes nothing else: arnx and arnx cloud work the same either way.

This website

  • arnx.dev is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • The website uses Google Analytics 4. It sets cookies and tells Google which pages you view, your browser and device type, and your approximate location. It also counts clicks on “Request a pilot invite” and contact links, with where the link sits on the page. It never receives what you type into a form. Google’s advertising features are off.
  • In the EU, the EEA and the UK, Google Analytics loads only after you click Accept in the cookie banner. Until then, nothing is sent to Google and no analytics cookies are set. To find out where you are, the site asks Cloudflare for your country; it is not stored. Your choice is kept in your browser only. Change it any time with “Cookies” at the bottom of the page; Reject also deletes the analytics cookies.
  • Elsewhere, Google Analytics loads after the page. You can block it with a browser extension or Google’s opt-out add-on.
  • The arnx tool never uses Google Analytics. Its only usage data is the opt-in telemetry described above.
  • If you email us or request a pilot invite (by email), we get your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to reply.

Who else handles data

  • Model providers: with arnx cloud, Anthropic, OpenAI and River, under their API terms. With your own keys, the providers you chose, under your own agreement with them. For arnx cloud we use only provider terms that do not let the provider train models on your code.
  • inet.ws: hosts arnx cloud, on a server in New York, USA.
  • GitHub: sign-in for arnx cloud, and website hosting.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • Cloudflare: DNS for arnx.dev, and delivery of this website. Traffic to api.arnx.dev does not pass through Cloudflare.
  • Google: Google Analytics, on this website only.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising. Data is processed in the USA and in the countries where these providers operate. When data moves from the EU, the EEA or the UK to those countries, we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum) or on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as each provider offers.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete your data, or to close your account. Email info@arnx.dev and we will reply within one week.

Why we use your data (the legal bases under the GDPR and the LGPD):

  • To provide arnx cloud (your account, reviews and credits): our contract with you.
  • Website analytics in the EU, the EEA and the UK, and telemetry everywhere: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Security, abuse prevention and checking our bills: our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • Payment records: legal obligations.

If you are in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Brazil, you can also restrict or object to our use of your data, and complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO; in Brazil, the ANPD). If you are in California, you can ask what we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it; we do not sell or share personal information.

arnx is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We will post changes here and update the date at the top. For important changes we will give 30 days’ notice, by email if you have an account.
