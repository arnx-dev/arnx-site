How credits work

Credits are your prepaid balance for arnx Cloud, in US dollars. Reviews spend credit as they run. A review temporarily reserves some of your balance as a hold. At the end, the hold is released and only what you used is charged.

Each review is billed what it used, from your prepaid USD credit. The CLI shows the charge per role so you can see where it went. arnx credits shows your balance and any amount reserved for reviews in progress.

How to top up

This page explains the process and takes no payment. When top-ups open, start in your terminal:

Balance and your top-up link 
arnx credits
  1. The CLI prints your balance and a Top up: link signed for your account.
  2. Open that link. It leads to api.arnx.dev/billing/topup, where you choose a pack. No browser sign-in is needed.
  3. Pay on Stripe Checkout.

The link is valid for one hour. Run arnx credits again any time for a fresh link. Keep it private: it is tied to your account. If an older link brought you here, use the CLI to get a fresh one when top-ups open.

Receipts and invoices

For each purchased pack, Stripe emails a receipt and a paid invoice with a PDF. You can add a business tax ID at checkout.
